# Theater: OREI EARC-EX165-K

The installed OREI pair carries television return audio and HDMI-CEC between the Sony and Denon over a single CAT6/7 run.

## Connections

```text
Sony HDMI IN 3 (eARC)
    -> OREI EARC-EX165-K TV-side unit
    -> CAT6/7
    -> OREI EARC-EX165-K AVR-side unit
    -> Denon MONITOR 1 HDMI OUT (eARC)
```

- Connect the **TV-side unit** to **Sony HDMI IN 3 (eARC)**.
- Join the OREI units with the installed **CAT6/7** run.
- Connect the **AVR-side unit** to **Denon MONITOR 1 HDMI OUT (eARC)**.
- Provide local power to the required **TV-side** and **AVR-side** units according to the product labeling on the hardware and included power supplies.

## Roles

- **eARC/ARC pass-through:** returns television audio to the Denon over the CAT6/7 link.
- **CEC pass-through:** preserves device discovery and control across the Sony-to-Denon path.

## Supported eARC formats

- LPCM 2.0
- LPCM 5.1
- LPCM 7.1
- Dolby Digital
- Dolby Digital Plus
- DTS
- Dolby TrueHD
- DTS-HD MA
- Dolby Atmos
