# Theater: Denon AVR-X4700H

The Denon is the theater's audio system. Follow the [room topology](../README.md#hdmi-topology).

## Connection and coordination

- Connect **Denon MONITOR 1 HDMI OUT (eARC)** to the AVPro transmitter at the equipment rack.
- The Sony returns audio through the extender. Ugoos video reaches the Sony directly.
- Apply the [Sony audio and BRAVIA Sync settings](sony-xr-65a90j.md), including `Speakers: Audio system` and `Sound mode sync: Off`.
- After installing the T2/R2 extender, refresh the Sony BRAVIA Sync list and verify that the Denon appears.

The original pilot guide did not specify Denon menu changes; record any required receiver settings here during installation.

## Audio validation

The [initial AVPro T/R pair](avpro-ac-ex40-444-plus.md) limits the return path to ARC. Full lossless validation must wait for the T2/R2 replacement.

Once eARC is active, apply the [Ugoos passthrough settings](ugoos-am6b-plus.md#audio-during-the-arc-only-interim). Verify that the Denon identifies a known TrueHD/Atmos track as **Dolby Atmos/TrueHD**, rather than Dolby Digital Plus or Dolby Surround. Record the result in the [room checklist](../README.md#room-validation).
